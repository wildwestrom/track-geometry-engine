Build more realistic railway alignments with the Track Geometry Tool.

When creating railways, the base game does not match the curvature at each tangent. If you've ever seen your trains suddenly "jerk" when changing direction or going from a curve to a straight section, this is why. In real life civil engineering, railway alignments are a chain of the following 3 primitives:

  • Straight Lines (in gray)
  • Circular Arcs (in blue)
  • Transition Spirals (in magenta, clothoids in this mod)

The transition spiral or transition curve is important, as it allows one to change the level of curvature more gradually. In real life, this translates to more stable operation, better passenger comfort, and less maintenance costs from wear and tear.

This mod is still early in development. If you find any issues or would like to see enhancements, you can send me a pull request at github.com/wildwestrom/track-geometry-engine/tree/tf3-mod. English only for now.

Quickstart:

  1. Select any track you want and turn on the track geometry tool.
  2. Click on the ground or snap to a track to start building.
  3. Drag the track wherever you like.
  4. Add curves by clicking on the yellow line.
  5. Click and drag the yellow circles where the yellow lines intersect. These points are called PIs (points of intersection).
  6. Right click a PI to delete it.
  7. Drag the green handles to adjust the curvature radius.
  8. Switch to profile mode to adjust vertical alignment.
  9. Click anywhere off the line to build.
  10. You can go do something else and come back and your progress will still be there.
  11. Turn the tool off to delete your progress and start over.
