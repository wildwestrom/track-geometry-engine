Build more realistic railway alignments with the Track Geometry Tool.

In the base game, all rails are roads are built from cubic Hermite splines. By default the game does not match curvature at tangents, so when riding your trains, you often see them suddenly "jerk". In real life civil engineering, railway alignments are a chain of the following 3 primitives:

  • Straight Lines
  • Circular Arcs
  • Transition Spirals (Clothoids in this mod)

The transition spiral or transition curve is important, as it allows one to change the level of curvature more gradually. In real life, this translates to more stable operation, a better passenger comfort, and less maintenance costs from wear and tear.
